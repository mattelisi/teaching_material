# Assets and sources

All 37 files in `figures/` are original redraws/plots generated by the included base R script `R/make_figures.R`. They use mathematical constructions or explicitly simulated data, not published experimental data. All original source assets remain in `../numerical_skills/`.

The reused external image in Part 2 is `assets/thura_2022_panel_a.png`, copied unchanged from the existing panel crop `numerical_skills/img/neuraltra_cisek.png`. The complete figure is also retained for context as `assets/thura_2022_figure4.png`, copied unchanged from `numerical_skills/img/journal.pbio.3001861.g004.PNG`.

Credit: Thura, D., Cabana, J.-F., Feghaly, A., & Cisek, P. (2022). *Integrated neural dynamics of sensorimotor decisions and actions*. PLOS Biology, 20(12), e3001861. [Article and Figure 4](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001861), [figure DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001861.g004). The article is distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The slide identifies the existing panel crop; no additional image editing was applied. Its axes are principal components of neural population activity; the teaching simulation does not reproduce the paper's analysis.

The Fourier R tutorial adapts the user-supplied `fourier_transform.m`. The PCA R tutorial adapts the [linked MATLAB example](https://github.com/mattelisi/NeuroMethods/blob/master/examples/PCA/pca_example.m), both from Matteo Lisi's teaching material. The lectures retain Matteo Lisi as author. The revisions and new plots are a draft for his review.

Quarto supplies the RevealJS presentation resources and its normal mathematical renderer. Mathematics may require an online connection; no separate equation-renderer download is required. Styles use system fonts rather than remote font imports.
